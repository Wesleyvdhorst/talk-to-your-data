AI Engineering Assignment: Talk With Your Data
Estimated time: 4-8 hours development time. We care more about depth of reflection and what you learned than feature count.
The task
Build a small "talk with your data" app: a user asks a question in natural language, the app figures out the answer from the dataset (typically by generating and running SQL), and explains the result.
Dataset: Olist Brazilian E-Commerce. Use at least these three tables: orders, order_reviews, and order_customers. Add more tables if it serves your app, your call.
Core requirements
	•	Load and model the data sensibly (your choice of storage; SQLite, DuckDB, Postgres, whatever)
	•	A working natural-language → answer loop (text-to-SQL is the expected baseline; if you choose a different approach, justify why)
	•	The app should handle ambiguous or broken questions gracefully; try to break it yourself and note what happens and explain why.
Bonus directions (pick what excites you, don't do all)
	•	A minimal frontend or interesting visualizations
	•	A trained model for a prediction task (review score, late delivery, churn, …)
	•	Hybrid SQL + semantic search over the free-text reviews
	•	A small eval set (5–10 questions with expected behavior) showing how your app performs
	•	Anything else you find interesting, surprise us
What we're evaluating
We care about process and learning at least as much as the final artifact:
	•	How you built it - fully manual, fully AI-assisted, hybrid? Which tools, which workflow, how did you manage context, did you use planning/subagents? There are better and worse ways to code with AI, show us you've thought about it, try something new and explain what you learned from this.
	•	Mastery of the codebase and concept – You don't need to have read every line, but you should be the conceptual owner: expect us to point at parts of the app and ask you to explain the details, control flow, and design choices.
	•	New skills - use at least one tool, library, technique, or AI engineering practice you haven't used before. Ideally more. What did you learn from it?
	•	Technical highlight - one or two things in the code, model, or feature you're proud of.
	•	Creativity & framing - what story does your app tell? What's interesting about it?
	•	Reflection - what broke or didn't work, what you'd do next with more time, what surprised you.
What we do not care about
Deployment, auth, polished UI, exhaustive documentation. Don't over-engineer.
Practical notes
	•	Vibecoding is encouraged. Use AI assistance freely and have fun with it but don’t outsource your brain.
	•	No agentic coding tool yet? Try the free tier of Google Antigravity. Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode etc. are all fine too.
	•	Need an LLM API key for the app itself? We can provide a temporary Claude Sonnet key, just ask.
Deliverable & presentation
	•	A repo (GitHub) with (runnable) code and a short README
	•	You'll present the app live (~60 min): walk us through what it does, how you built it, your AI-assisted workflow, the technical highlight, and what you learned. No formal write-up or screen recording needed, but feel free to jot notes to structure your thoughts around the evaluation criteria above.
Have fun with it.
